LabSaver Chrome Extension

LabSaver makes it easy to export your lab results from Function Health and Sutter Health directly to Google Sheets, giving you complete control over your health data.

Key Features

  • ✅ Export lab results from Function Health and Sutter Health
  • ✅ Direct Google Sheets integration
  • ✅ One-click export process
  • ✅ Organize and track results over time
  • ✅ Privacy-focused - no data stored by the extension

How It Works

  1. Log into your Function Health or Sutter Health account
  2. Navigate to test results. An "Export Labs" will appear.
  3. Click the "Export Labs" button at the top of the page.
  4. Authorize Google Sheets access (one time)
  5. Your results are instantly exported to your spreadsheet

Privacy & Security

  • ✅ All data processing happens locally in your browser
  • ✅ Results go directly to YOUR Google Sheets account
  • ✅ You maintain complete control of your health data
  • ❌ No data collection or storage on our servers

What Gets Exported?

  • Test names and values
  • Units of measurement
  • Reference ranges
  • Out-of-range indicators
  • Test dates and timestamps
  • Provider information
  • LOINC codes for standardization where possible

Feature Details

1. Comprehensive Data Export

  • All lab results with complete history
  • Reference ranges and normal values
  • Test dates and ordering information
  • Organized into multiple sheets for easy analysis

2. Smart Organization

  • Labs from both services can be saved in one Google Sheet
  • Creates a Contents overview tab of what is included
  • Creates 4 sheets of data: Export, Definitions, Latest, Table view
  • Makes it easy to share with your doctor or favorite LLM

3. One-Click Export

  • Simple button appears on Function Health and Sutter Health pages
  • Automatic Google authentication
  • Progress tracking during export

What Gets Added to Google Sheet

Function Health export:

  • FH_Values: All lab results with full history
  • FH_Definitions: Biomarker definitions and reference ranges
  • FH_Latest: Most recent value for each biomarker
  • FH_Table: Pivot table view (biomarkers × dates)
  • FH_Grouped: Results grouped by category

Sutter Health export:

  • SH_Values: All lab results with full history
  • SH_Definitions: Biomarker definitions and reference ranges
  • SH_Latest: Most recent value for each biomarker
  • SH_Table: Pivot table view (biomarkers × dates)
  • SH_Grouped: Results grouped by category
